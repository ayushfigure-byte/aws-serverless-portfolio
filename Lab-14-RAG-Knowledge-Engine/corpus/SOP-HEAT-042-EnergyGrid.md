# NOAA SOP-HEAT-042: Regional Grid Stress & Heat Action Protocol
**Classification:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Applies To:** Regional Grid Operators, NOAA Field Stations, NWS Liaisons

## 1. Trigger Conditions
* **Level 1 (Heat Advisory):** Daily maximum temperature $T_{max} \ge 95^\circ\text{F}$ for 2 consecutive days, or $z$-score $\ge 1.5\sigma$.
* **Level 2 (Severe Heat Warning):** $T_{max} \ge 104^\circ\text{F}$, or $z$-score $\ge 2.5\sigma$.
* **Level 3 (Emergency Power Mitigation):** $T_{max} \ge 108^\circ\text{F}$ combined with regional load forecast exceeding 92% available capacity.

## 2. Mandatory Action Matrix
* **Level 1 Actions:**
  * Notify regional transmission operators (RTOs) at 06:00 local time.
  * Defer non-critical transmission line maintenance.
* **Level 2 Actions:**
  * Enact rolling transformer substation cooling via auxiliary spray units.
  * Issue public industrial power-shedding requests for peak hours (14:00 - 18:00).
  * Activate municipal cooling shelter networks.
* **Level 3 Actions:**
  * Authorize emergency generation spinning reserves.
  * Direct 5% voltage reduction (brownout prevention) across non-critical distribution loops.
