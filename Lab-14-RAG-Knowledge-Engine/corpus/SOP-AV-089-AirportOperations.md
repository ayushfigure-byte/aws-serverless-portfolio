# NOAA SOP-AV-089: High-Density Altitude & Surface Heat Aviation Protocol
**Classification:** Operational Guidance Bulletin  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Applies To:** Air Traffic Control Liaisons, Commercial Airport Duty Officers

## 1. Density Altitude Risk Thresholds
When surface temperatures exceed $102^\circ\text{F}$ ($38.9^\circ\text{C}$):
1. **Takeoff Runway Distance:** Increase calculated takeoff ground roll by 3.5% per $1^\circ\text{F}$ above standard atmosphere.
2. **Payload Weight Restrictions:** Mandatory maximum takeoff weight (MTOW) recalculations for regional jets (e.g., CRJ-700/900, E175).
3. **Ramp Safety:** Restrict continuous ground personnel tarmac exposure to 20-minute operational intervals with mandatory 40-minute hydration cycles.

## 2. Runway Surface Integrity
* Asphalt runway surface temperatures exceeding $140^\circ\text{F}$ require physical tire adhesion inspections every 90 minutes.
* Brake cooling times post-landing must be extended by a factor of 1.4x.
